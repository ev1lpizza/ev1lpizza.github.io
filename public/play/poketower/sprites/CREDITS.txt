Pokémon sprites (sprites/pokemon/)
  Source: PokeAPI sprite collection, https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites
  Files are named after each Pokémon, for example "mr-mime.png".
  The Electric, Rock, Ground, Poison, Flying, and Ice Pokémon added later
  (Mareep to Abomasnow) come from the same collection (sprites/pokemon/<number>.png).
  snorlax.png (the sleeping Snorlax mystery event) is the collection's
  sprites/pokemon/143.png.

Professor Oak, trainer classes, and gym leaders (sprites/trainers/)
  Source: Pokémon Showdown, https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/
  kindler.png is Showdown's "kindler-gen3.png".
  guitarist.png, hiker.png, ruinmaniac.png, biker.png, birdkeeper.png, and
  skier.png are Showdown's files of the same names.
  rival.png (your rival) is Showdown's "blue.png".
