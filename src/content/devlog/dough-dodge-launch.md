---
title: "Dough Dodge: tuning a one-screen dodger"
date: 2026-10-21
summary: Hit-stop, screen shake and why the pepperoni falls slower than the pins.
game: dough-dodge
---

Dough Dodge is about one idea: a tiny slice of pizza in a kitchen that wants it dead.

The thing that made it feel good was **hit-stop**. When a rolling pin hits you, the game freezes for about a tenth of a second before the screen shakes. It's short enough that you don't notice it as a pause, but it makes every hit land.

Pepperoni falls a little slower than the pins. That gives you a reason to move toward danger instead of hiding in a corner, and it's where most of the decisions in a run come from.
