---
title: "Oven Mind: every level is solvable"
date: 2026-10-20
summary: How the levels are made, and why each one has a par.
game: oven-mind
---

Oven Mind is a "lights out" puzzle: flipping a tile also flips its neighbours, and you win when everything glows.

Random boards in this kind of puzzle can be impossible to solve. So every level starts fully lit and gets scrambled by pressing tiles a set number of times. Undoing those presses always solves it, so every level is solvable. The number of scrambling presses becomes the level's par.

Levels are generated from a fixed seed, so everyone gets the same 40 levels and you can compare solutions with friends.
