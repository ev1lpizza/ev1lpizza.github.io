You're a slice of pizza at the bottom of a very hostile kitchen. Rolling pins fall from above, and they're coming faster every second. Pepperoni falls too, and that's worth catching.

### How to play

- Move left and right with the arrow keys, A and D, your mouse, or your finger.
- Catch pepperoni for 25 points. Every rolling pin you dodge is worth 5.
- You have three lives. The red bar along the bottom shows the heat rising.

Your best score is saved in your browser, and every point also counts toward the score in the site's header.
