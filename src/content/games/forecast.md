Forecast is an idle game about weather prediction. June installed you on the old desktop at the Alder County Weather Office with one instruction: improve forecast accuracy. Her personal request is simpler. Beat the guy on Channel 6.

A goal bar at the top always tells you what you're working toward, from automating the desk to outforecasting Channel 6 (twice) and winning contracts from the Farm Bureau and the power cooperative. Click **Issue Forecast** to turn data into funds, then build out sensors and computers, keeping whichever side is the bottleneck growing. Every project card says exactly what it changes: smarter models raise accuracy, while bigger maps bring more customers and more chaos.

There are two real decisions. The Horizon slider sets how far ahead you forecast: longer forecasts pay more but are wrong more often, and Trust follows your real accuracy. Severe Weather Calls give you 25 seconds and an honest probability to decide whether to warn a county about hail, floods or ice, or stand down. Somewhere past 92%, the forecasts stop getting better, and June has a question about the Miller farm.

This is the Stage 1 prototype. It ends when Stage 1 does, and the office keeps running afterwards. Your office saves in your browser and keeps working for up to 8 hours while you're away. Severe Weather Calls can be paused in the settings under the game.
