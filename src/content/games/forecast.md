Forecast is an idle game about weather prediction. June installed you on the old desktop at the Alder County Weather Office and asked you to beat the guy on Channel 6. Click **Issue Forecast** to turn data into funds, then spend them on rain gauges, volunteer observers and computers, keeping whichever side is the bottleneck growing.

Projects make your model smarter, widen your coverage across the map and win contracts, but every bigger map brings more chaos. The Horizon slider is the main decision: longer forecasts pay more and are wrong more often, and Trust follows your real accuracy. Somewhere past 92%, the forecasts stop getting better, and June has a question about the Miller farm.

This is the Stage 1 prototype. It ends when Stage 1 does, and the office keeps running afterwards. Your office saves in your browser and keeps working for up to 8 hours while you're away.
