A calm puzzle about heat. Tapping a tile flips it and its four neighbours between cold and glowing. Light up the whole oven to clear the level.

### How to play

- Click, tap, or move with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space to flip a tile.
- Every level can be solved, and each one has a par: the number of moves the oven was scrambled with.
- Press R to restart a level, or use the buttons under the board.

There are 40 levels and they get bigger as you go. Your progress is saved in your browser.
