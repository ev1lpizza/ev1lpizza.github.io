PokéTower is a roguelike deckbuilder in the spirit of Slay the Spire, set in the world of Pokémon. Pick a Fire, Water or Grass starter, then climb a branching map of wild battles, trainers, shops, mystery rooms and Nurse stations. Every Pokémon's moves are cards: build your deck as you go, read what the enemy is about to do, and play the right cards at the right time.

Each floor ends with a Kanto gym leader. Beat them to earn a badge and evolve your team. Beat the third and you win the run. Lose your whole party and you start again from scratch, though your Pokédex and unlocked difficulty levels carry over.

Your run, Pokédex and settings save in your browser, so you can close the tab and come back later.
